Tutorials

Step-by-step guides — pick the one that matches where you are

1

Install a wallet

Get Phantom or Solflare from the official store. Create a new wallet or import an existing one with your seed phrase. Write the seed phrase down on paper — never store it digitally.

2

Fund your wallet

Buy SOL on an exchange and send it to your wallet address, or receive from a friend. You need at least 0.05 SOL for the anti-sybil check — the balance never leaves your wallet.

3

Connect to SolDrop

Click Connect at the top of the page. Your extension popup opens — approve the connection. Your wallet address appears and quest 1 is marked done.

4

Verify holdings

Press Verify. The site reads your on-chain balance — if you hold ≥ 0.05 SOL the check passes instantly. No signature needed for this step.

5

Join the community

Press Open X and follow @solana. Come back and the quest auto-completes after a few seconds. This proves you're a real human, not a bot.

6

Claim your 1 SOL

With all 3 quests done, the CLAIM button lights up. Press it, approve the signature in your wallet, and 1.00 SOL arrives instantly. Verify the transaction in the Solana explorer.

FAQ

Quick answers to the common ones

Is SolDrop legit?

Yes. SolDrop runs seasonal ecosystem drops funded by partner protocols. Every claim is an on-chain transaction you can verify in the Solana explorer. Never share your seed phrase — a real claim only ever asks for a signature.

Why do I need 0.05 SOL?

Anti-sybil. Empty wallets are almost always bots farming drops. The threshold proves you're a real user. Your balance never leaves your wallet during verification.

Which wallets work?

Any Solana browser extension: Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, Glow. Mobile users can open soldrop inside their wallet's built-in browser.

When do I get my 1 SOL?

Instantly after the final signature — the claim transaction sends it straight to the wallet you connected with.

I need help — where is support?

Hit the Support button bottom-right. Our AI assistant answers instantly, and a human picks up anything it can't solve.
